Features of AvioTest®

AvioTest is cross-platform and built for flexible study. Applicants can train anywhere, anytime, and offline without an internet connection. For PACE, the real exam is delivered on a touchscreen, so the software mirrors that experience and lets candidates practice on touch-enabled devices for a more realistic exam day feel.

AvioTest supports joystick-operated tests on both macOS and Windows devices, allowing applicants to practice with flight-style controls when required. The training approach guides you through 21 focused modules step by step for gradual performance improvements. To improve training quality, the software includes progression graphs and detailed score analysis so you can track your development over time. Each module provides performance breakdowns that highlight speed, accuracy, and consistency, helping you identify weak areas, focus your training, and build measurable progress for Turkish Airlines PACE, sunexpress pilot yetiştirme, sunexpress pilotluk eğitimi, and Pegasus pilot yetiştirme goals.
