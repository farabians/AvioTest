Success Tips for Pegasus CASE Assessment

By AvioTest Editorial • January 15, 2026 • 5 min read

If you're applying for a cadet pilot position at Pegasus Airlines, you'll likely encounter the CASE assessment. This comprehensive suite of tests evaluates everything from your technical aptitude to your personality and multi-tasking abilities.

The Core of CASE: Spatial & Vigilance

Pegasus places a heavy emphasis on your ability to maintain situational awareness. The Spatial Orientation 2D and 3D modules are legendary for their difficulty. You'll need to rapidly interpret various instrument readings and translate them into a coherent mental model of where your aircraft is in space.

"Vigilance isn't just about watching; it's about noticing the subtle changes before they become problems."

Vigilance Modules: Staying Sharp

The CASE assessment includes two types of vigilance tests. One focuses on your ability to monitor multiple sources of information over a long period, while the other tests your reaction speed to sudden, infrequent events. Training your brain to stay "locked in" for these tests is vital.

Why Multi-Tasking is King

In the Pegasus assessment, you're rarely just doing one thing. You might be solving math problems while simultaneously monitoring a radar screen and responding to audio cues. This is where most candidates struggle. Using a dedicated platform like AvioTest® allows you to practice these exact combinations of tasks, building the required mental "bandwidth."

Preparation Checklist

  • Instrument Reading: Brush up on your knowledge of ALTIMETER, HSI, and VSI readings.
  • Mental Math: Practice speed calculations involving percentages, conversions, and fuel burn rates.
  • Endurance: The full assessment can last several hours. Practice long sessions to build mental stamina.

Preparing for Pegasus CASE is a marathon, not a sprint. By focusing on your spatial awareness and resilience through dedicated practice, you'll put yourself in the best possible position to join the Pegasus family.
