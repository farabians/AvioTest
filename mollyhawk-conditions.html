SunExpress Application Requirements

Mollyhawk Pilot Training Program (Cadet)

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent.
  • No archived criminal record.
  • Applicants who have not previously participated in an MPL screening or interview session at SunExpress are eligible to apply.

Candidates should demonstrate English proficiency by meeting one of the following criteria:

TOEIC

Reading: 385, Listening: 400, Speaking: 160, Writing: 150

TOEFL IBT

Reading: 21, Listening: 21, Speaking: 22, Writing: 20

IELTS (Academic)

Reading: 6.5, Listening: 6, Speaking: 6.5, Writing: 6

ITEP Academic Plus

Reading: 3.5, Listening: 3.5, Speaking: 3.5, Writing: 3.5

Ability to meet the requirements for obtaining a valid Class 1 health certificate in accordance with Part-MED standards.
