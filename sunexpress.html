SunExpress Assessment Details

Education & Medical

  • Bachelor's Degree: Applicants must have at least an undergraduate degree.
  • Medical Certificate: Possession of a valid Class 1 Medical Certificate is required.
  • Age Performance: Maximum age requirements usually apply for the MPL program.

English Proficiency

One of the following scores must be provided for the application:

  • TOEIC: Listening (400), Reading (385)
  • TOEFL iBT: 75 (L: 20, R: 18, S: 20, W: 18)
  • IELTS: 6.0 (Each module min. 5.5)
  • ITEP Academic Plus: 3.5 (Each section min. 3.5)
