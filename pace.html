What is the PACE Test?

The psychometric assessment system introduced in recent years is called PACE. This test aims to measure candidates' technical competencies and is conducted within the Turkish Airlines Flight Training Department. The application lasts approximately 5–6 hours. This test consists of 2 knowledge and 7 ability modules.

Ability Modules

Ability modules are intended to evaluate the fundamental cognitive and psychomotor skills required for a pilot. In this context, critical competencies such as spatial perception, memory, psychomotor, and attention coordination are measured.

The main modules included in the assessment process are as follows:

Audio Visual Memory Measures the capacity to perceive and remember visual and auditory information simultaneously.
Verbal Memory – Digit Span Evaluates the retention of numerical information in short-term memory and its correct recall.
3D Spatial Perception Measures the perception of objects in three-dimensional space and interprets spatial relationships.
Spatial Orientation Evaluates the ability to correctly analyze direction, position, and movement relationships.
Sustained Attention Measures the ability to maintain attention levels over long periods.
Cognitive Agility Evaluates the ability to adapt quickly to changing situations and perform mental transitions effectively.
Psychomotor Coordination and Multi-tasking Ability Measures hand-eye coordination and the capacity to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.

Knowledge Modules

Knowledge modules are applied to evaluate the basic Mathematics and Physics levels of candidates. These modules aim to measure analytical thinking skills and the level of understanding of basic scientific information.

The main modules included in the assessment process are as follows:

Mathematics Analytical thinking ability, basic mathematical operations, and aviation-focused problem-solving skills.
Physics Measures the theoretical and practical application level of mechanics, thermodynamics, and basic physics principles.
