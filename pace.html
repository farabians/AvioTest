PACE Exam Structure

The PACE Exam is made up of 9 modules. The exam is completed in three sections, with 10 minute breaks in between.

The 3 Sections Pattern

The PACE exam is completed in three distinct sections with breaks.

PACE Exam Section 1
→
PACE Exam Section 2
→
PACE Exam Section 3

Section 1 — 3 Modules

  • Sustained Attention
  • Audio-Visual Memory
  • Digit Span
  • Break: 10-minute break follows this section.

Section 2 — 3 Modules

  • Agility
  • Three-Dimensional Spatial Perception
  • Spatial Orientation
  • Break: 10-minute break follows this section.

Section 3 — 3 Modules

  • Physics
  • Mathematics
  • MIC (Joystick Module)
  • End: This section completes the PACE exam.

Note: There are 10-minute breaks between each section.
